Comarch Loyalty Management

Backoffice · Desktop & Mobile

UX/UI design for enterprise SaaS: loyalty backoffice, e-invoicing, and mobile loyalty apps. Research-led flows, WCAG audits, and a design system built for scale and production hand-off.

Sketch — design and symbol libraries before the team standardised on Figma. Figma — production screens, flows, and component specs for backoffice and mobile. FigJam — journey mapping and alignment with product and engineering. WCAG plugins — contrast, focus, and labelling checks on loyalty and invoicing surfaces. Maze — moderated validation on priority flows. Claude & AI Factory — documentation, design-system hygiene, and AI-assisted spec drafting. Confluence, Jira & GitHub — UX specs and hand-off documentation for cross-functional teams.

Product context

Enterprise SaaS for loyalty programme management, e-invoicing, and mobile apps — operators moving through dense data and multi-step setup under time pressure.

  • Desktop — programme setup, member management, rewards, reporting, e-invoicing.
  • Mobile — programme monitoring, member lookup, field-ready actions.

UX practice

  • Discovery — priority journeys: loyalty rules, member accounts, invoicing hand-offs.
  • Deliverables — annotated flows, edge-case maps, sprint-ready specs.
  • Mobile — task-focused paths, not shrunken desktop tables.
  • Focus — consistent status language, progressive disclosure, shared tokens across platforms.

Systems & delivery

  • Design system — component library restructure: semantic naming, tokens, AI-ready hand-off.
  • Accessibility — WCAG audits on live surfaces; UI, copy, and focus states fixed before development.
  • Hand-off — Figma libraries, interaction notes, edge-case documentation.
  • Release — upgraded loyalty and invoicing on desktop and mobile.

Agile workflow

  • Collaboration — with product, engineering, and QA through discovery, design critiques, and walkthroughs — iterating on flows and specs as features developed.
  • Tracking — specs in Confluence, tickets in Jira, assets in GitHub.
  • Quality bar — accessibility checks as a standard ship criterion.

Impact

UX

Clearer paths for operators — I mapped how people actually work across loyalty and invoicing, so programme setup, member tasks, and moving between modules are easier to follow.

Org-wide

Component library restructuring — AI-ready naming, tokens, and hand-off adopted across product teams, with regular demo reviews to keep design, product, and engineering aligned.

Pipeline

WCAG in the design workflow — accessibility audits on live product surfaces, then UI patterns, copy, and focus states reworked before development — not patched after release.

Product

Upgraded loyalty & invoicing — clearer flows and expanded functionality on desktop and mobile.

Metrics and screen visuals are confidential under NDA. Outcomes reflect sustained UX ownership — from research through production on enterprise SaaS.
