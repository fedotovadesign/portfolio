Confluence, Jira & GitHub — UX specs and hand-off documentation for cross-functional teams. Sketch — design and symbol libraries before the team standardised on Figma.

Product context

Enterprise SaaS platform for loyalty programme management and customer-facing mobile apps, designed for business clients such as petrol stations. The backoffice empowers operators handling dense data and complex configurations under tight deadlines, while the mobile apps engage end customers throughout their shopping, earning, and reward redemption journey.

  • Desktop — programme setup, member management, rewards, reporting.
  • Mobile — online purchases, earning and redeeming rewards, multi-programme support.

UX practice

  • Discovery — loyalty rules and member accounts.
  • Deliverables — annotated flows, edge-case maps, sprint specs.
  • Mobile — task-focused paths.

Systems & delivery

  • Design system — semantic naming, tokens, AI-ready hand-off.
  • Accessibility — WCAG audits, library updates.
  • Hand-off — Figma libraries, interaction notes, edge-case docs.
  • Demo review — UX/UI alignment with shipped design.

Agile workflow

  • Collaboration — discovery, design critiques, walkthroughs with product, engineering, QA.
  • Tracking — specs in Confluence, tickets in Jira, assets in GitHub.
  • Quality bar — accessibility as standard ship criterion.

Impact

UX

Operator journey mapping — traced actual workflows across loyalty operations to simplify programme setup, member management, and cross-module navigation.

Org-wide

Component library restructuring — established AI-ready design tokens and handover standards across product teams, with regular demo reviews to keep design, product, and engineering in sync.

Pipeline

Accessibility built into design — WCAG audits and library updates.

Product

Loyalty programme upgrade — clearer user flows and expanded capabilities across both desktop and mobile platforms.

Metrics and screen visuals are confidential under NDA. Outcomes reflect sustained UX ownership — from research through production on enterprise SaaS.
